==================================================
15. STUDENT MINUTE SYSTEM
==================================================

Students do NOT have a normal monetary wallet.

Students have a TIME BALANCE.

Store time internally in:

SECONDS

Example:

56 minutes = 3360 seconds

Display:

56:00

During a call:

56:00
55:59
55:58
...

Time must be deducted by the actual duration.

Example:

Call duration:

2 minutes 37 seconds

Deduct:

157 seconds

NOT 3 minutes.

The server must be authoritative.

Never trust frontend-only timers.
